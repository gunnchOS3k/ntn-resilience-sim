# Scientific limitations and threats to validity

**Originality risk:** elementary threshold/availability derivations and a transparent reproduction audit may not clear OJ-COMS or Systems Journal originality expectations. Existing disaster simulators and rich NTN evaluations make broad novelty claims untenable. Owner review should decide whether to seek independent technical review or expand evidence before submission.

**Construct validity:** useful service is one configured1Mbps/500ms conjunction. It is not verified application completion. Available means Bernoulli path state, not attachment or packet delivery. Uptime can be high while service fails. Recovery is the first miss-to-restoration only, with no specified step duration.

**Internal validity:** policies have perfect instantaneous information. Adaptive uses qualification while simple priority baselines do not. Switching is instantaneous and cost-free. TN always qualifies. Its dominance/equivalence is partly designed into the model. The power gate is shared, with no separate backup power or energy consumption. Unknown assumptions cannot be repaired by more seeds.

**External validity:** one synthetic scenario; independent memoryless events; no traffic, capacity sharing, queues, geometry, weather, RF calibration, gateways, core/backhaul failure, correlated restoration, or physical device logs. The named scenario does not establish a geographic population. Related host-device evidence from other repositories is not NTN validation.

**Statistical validity:**30short timelines, rounded fractions, repeated random streams across grids and probes; no multiplicity-controlled inference. Two unfavorable probes instantiate the same mathematical condition. Confidence intervals quantify fixed-model sampling only. A population ceiling is not a finite-run ceiling. The source effect-size helper's zero-SD convention is not mathematically interpreted.

**Historical provenance:** four-seed manuscript means are stale historical summaries. Current tables use30seeds from source05aaa9a, seed-parity revision7afde2e. Frozen files claim predeclaration; this continuation did not establish independent protocol-before-outcome chronology, so no independently registered experiment is claimed.

**Evidence missing for stronger claims:** measured delay/goodput/power and service completion; justified workloads; realistic correlated failures; independently powered NTN; traffic-dependent feeder capacity; switching/attachment cost; held-out conditions; equivalent policy information and budgets; independent scientific novelty assessment. These are future studies. The existing bounded synthetic counterexample does not require inventing them.

**Declarations:** additional authors, contributions, funding/sponsors, conflicts, IP/rights, dissemination history, and human verification remain pending. No IRB exemption, institutional approval, Oulu relationship, acceptance, or submission is asserted.
