# Paper III originality and scientific-strength assessment

The defensible contribution is narrow: an exactly reproducible threshold-qualified two-path engine, elementary analytical boundaries, a useful-service versus uptime distinction, preserved counterexamples and a shared-power ceiling. It is not the first disaster-aware TN-NTN simulator, new satellite control, a novel optimization algorithm or a calibrated radio model.

Wang2026 already reports disaster-aware integrated-system simulation. Figaro2026 reports protocol-aware NTN performance with 3GPP calibration. Their presence prevents broad novelty assertions. The current benchmark is more tractable and easier to audit, but tractability alone does not establish journal-level originality. Its mathematical expectations are elementary probability calculations, and its favorable equivalence is built into instantaneous qualified-path selection.

An owner/specialist must judge whether this transparency/negative-result case is sufficient for OJ-COMS, or whether a methods note, education companion or a newly registered richer study is more appropriate. That decision remains open. A richer study would justify workload thresholds, include correlated failures, independent backup power, capacity sharing and switch penalties, and compare reproducible baselines or calibrated traces. No such work is represented as completed here.
