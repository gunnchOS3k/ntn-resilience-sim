# Abstract

Scenario-Based NTN Resilience Simulation for Digitally Underserved and Disrupted Communities presents a **research prototype** artifact for community-scale 6G research. Contribution focus: scenario schema, outage/fallback lifecycle, resilience metrics, ethical framing.
Results reported here are from **preliminary toy demonstrations** using synthetic data only; field validation is future work.
We do not claim operational 6G deployment, Oulu/CWC affiliation, or carrier-grade performance.
