# Paper III prospective correlated TN/NTN application-resilience study — P4 draft v0.1

Question: Under traffic-dependent service and correlated outages,when does independent terminal/gateway power improve application service compared with shared power,and when do switching/congestion erase the benefit of NTN fallback? Primary hypothesis: independent backup power architecture improves paired completed-task fraction by a practically useful0.02 in a predeclared compound-outage mixture. Primary practical-superiority null: paired architecture improvement<=0.02; planning alternative 0.04 (gap 0.02). This is an architecture comparison with an explicitly different power resource,not a fair controller-superiority effect.

Primary endpoint: correct,deadline/freshness-qualified completed tasks / ALL exogenous scheduled eligible tasks over a fixed episode. Secondary: outage duration distribution,recovery latency,time to resynchronization,queue/drop/congestion,terminal/gateway availability,power-duration/energy,failed handovers,selected-path uptime and per-service task coverage. Scalar qualification remains a diagnostic to preserve continuity with the old model; it cannot stand in for task completion.

Methods/layers: first a dimensioned system-level dependency/queue model with a shared exogenous event schedule and explicit architecture graph. Protocol-level ns-3/NR-NTN validation is a subsequent separately budgeted stage requiring versioned calibration and scenario compatibility; it is not implied by the analytical model. Real RF/satellite/operator measurements require separate equipment/access/data rights and are not claimed.

Truth/input distributions: continuous-time or slot-duration-specified semi-Markov outage intervals; common disaster/power parent plus conditional TN backhaul,edge,NTN gateway,terminal and visibility events. Proposed holding-time families exponential(primary) and heavy-tailed heldout,with medians fixed before outcomes. Correlation parameter controls a common-cause fraction of events (not casually called a Pearson coefficient). Include0,0.5,0.9 common-cause fractions as authored stress settings,not fitted disaster frequencies. NTN visibility from explicitly authored pass windows or an authorized,versioned ephemeris model; no invented commercial traces. Separate terminal vs remote-gateway failure so independence has a defined graph. Proposed shared/independent terminal power configurations hold network/workload inputs paired,account extra battery energy/cost instead of hiding it.

Traffic/capacity: multiple simultaneous flows,finite gateway/spotbeam capacity and scheduler; task bytes,request timing and deadlines explicit. Low/near-capacity/overload at proposed offered-load ratios0.3/0.8/1.2,with bursty arrivals held out. All ratios refer to declared aggregate capacity,not guaranteed per-terminal service. Queue delay feeds one-way end-to-end application delay; RTT and propagation are separate named fields. GEO-like and LEO-like scalar bounds are synthetic stress configurations until link-budget/geometry calibration. Include a visible path that fails deadline or capacity,unavailable gateway,shared power loss,independent power depletion and a switch whose interruption exceeds the remaining deadline.

Policies: TN-only; satellite-first; TN-first availability fallback; qualification-aware fallback; hysteresis-aware qualification. Same observations,action validator,terminal/gateway capacity and resource budgets within each architecture. Primary architecture contrast uses the SAME qualification/hysteresis policy in each architecture,not a different adaptive rule. Controller contrasts are secondary with multiplicity control. Include a matched make-before-break idealization only as privileged reference with extra resources labeled.

Constraints/actions: one active access unless dual-radio hardware budget is explicitly approved; migration/setup consumes capacity,time and energy; terminal battery and gateway UPS states evolve. Storage/local/peer modes remain absent unless a new named adapter and outcome protocol are implemented; no extrapolation from the old power ceiling. Shared-power old proof only bounds expected service under its common gate. Independent power or gateway topology requires a new bound. Common-cause probability bounds and queue stability/switch-delay conditions are mathematical targets,not completed new theorems.

Scenario/holdout: freeze one primary outage/workload mixture including favorable,neutral and harmful configurations; do not make all selected NTN paths qualify. Reserve different outage-duration tails,overlapping gateway/pass outages and burst/congestion levels. Do not estimate mixture weights from observed policy rankings. Pilot/paired seed units and fixed-n plan as below (hypotheticalSD 0.15,delta 0.02,half-width 0.01,cap2500). Proposed fixed horizon 600 dimensioned seconds per episode; network/service parameters require sensitivity and calibration before execution.

Resources/stop: initial system-level study proposed 8 CPU-hours; protocol-level calibration separately budgeted up to 24 CPU-hours only with new approval; no satellite account or paid trace access now. Stop before holdout on invalid probability/dependency/scheduling/time units,unbalanced architecture energy accounting or inadequate sample precision within cap. Runtime congestion,outage and depleted power are legitimate outcomes,not bugs to remove. Record every seed,dependency event,allocation,queue,task,transition and energy trajectory.

Physical priority: controlled terminal power loss and independent backup power with measured application request/response and gateway dependency,plus a terrestrial link emulator. A real NTN terminal and licensed service/pass observations are optional separate validations with permissions; never substitute authored pass windows for actual satellite visibility. User-participant usefulness assessment is distinct from scripted service correctness.

## Statistical and reproducibility rules
Use one primary paired contrast, a fixed exogenous workload/scenario mixture and whole-seed/session means. Each policy/model receives the same exogenous inputs in its paired unit; policy-dependent queues/batteries evolve separately. Never pool this study with historical generators. Correctness checks and pilot/training samples are excluded from confirmatory inference. Primary two-sided95% paired Student-t interval, sample SD(ddof1) and paired differences; disclose distribution/dependence assumptions and influential pairs. Secondary bootstrap sensitivity is descriptive at the independent unit; do not resample slots/tasks as independent when clustered. Zero-crossing is inconclusive, not equivalence. Secondary confirmatory families use Holm; remaining subgroups are explicitly exploratory. Report all planned outcomes, harms, missing/invalid actions, timeouts and precision failures.

Planning example uses a hypothetical paired SD 0.15, not measured/calculated from old means. For practical delta 0.02,power 0.90 and two-sided alpha 0.05, normal planning gives approximately592paired units; half-width 0.01 needs865. Use iterative t-quantile planning in Paper II tooling; a40-unit prospective development pilot would estimate SD and select n before any heldout outcome is read, minimum 30, maximum 2500. Starting expectation is about 900units if the hypothetical variance holds, not a fixed guarantee of power. If required n exceeds cap or resource estimate, stop before heldout and revise/seek approval. Pilot variance selection has uncertainty; archive that estimate and sensitivity. These are proposed future counts; no seeds are expanded now. Do not append units after viewing heldout results.

Derive disjoint pilot/training/heldout seed streams from approved study/config SHA and split/index using SHA256; archive actual seed lists and input hashes only in the separately authorized milestone. No reserved future seed list or outcome is created here. Freeze policies, tuning bounds, scenario mixture, n and stopping/multiplicity rules before heldout. An independent reviewer should audit the freeze and holdout access log. Archive source/config SHAs, dependencies, hardware/runtime, observations/actions/rejections/compute/cost traces, raw task outcomes, seed-level summaries, independent analysis, data dictionary and SHA256SUMS. A checksum establishes bytes, not validity or ownership.

Human decisions: approve science/effect size/sampling and device/workload assumptions; settle rights/permissions, intended claims, required coauthors/contributions/funding/conflicts and patent/IP concerns; authorize a separate execution milestone. ## Practical-effect clarification
Primary practical success requires the lower 95% paired interval to exceed 0.02; planning uses hypothetical alternative 0.04 against margin 0.02 (gap 0.02). An interval containing 0 is inconclusive about improvement,not equivalence; upper bound below 0 indicates harm. Positive but margin-straddling intervals do not establish practical success. Preserve neutral/harmful power,capacity and switching regimes. These rules are prospective proposals,not results.

P4 statistical-design clarification: the generic sample-size illustration below uses an effect GAP of 0.02; when testing practical superiority over0.02,the planning alternative is0.04. It must not be read as 90% power to demonstrate an effect greater than0.02when the true effect equals0.02. Actual effect/margin choices require human/domain justification before freezing.
